# OFFBEAT
A simple browser-based html game that is subjected around an Apples to Apples like game. It has online multiplayer capabilities with custom matchmaking and room specific leaderboards. It's unique because on top of having your default cards, you can also have AI generate a card for you, or you can even write your own custom card!

# How to Install/Play
Press 'Code', then 'Download ZIP', then, once the zip has been downloaded, unzip it or extract it and open the throw.html. It will open the game in a new tab in your default browser.

# How It Works
Basically, it's just online multiplayer Apples to Apples or Cards Against Humanity. It's quite fun and interesting. This game is also family-friendly (unless you write something bad on a custom card).
